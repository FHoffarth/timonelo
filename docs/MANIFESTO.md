# Timonelo Manifesto

---

## 1. What Timonelo Is

Timonelo is an independent cruise knowledge platform.

Its purpose is to help passengers understand their ship before boarding:

- Find their cabin
- Understand where it sits in physical space
- Explore the ship's 3D anatomy
- Inspect verified directional photos
- Understand multi-deck layouts
- Eliminate pre-voyage uncertainty

The product experience is modeled after spatial systems like **Google Maps, Apple Maps, and Airbnb**, focusing entirely on orientation, physical clarity, and verifiable facts.

---

## 2. What Timonelo Is NOT

To preserve objectivity, Timonelo enforces strict negative boundaries:

- **NOT a booking engine**: We do not sell inventory, track fares, or optimize conversions.
- **NOT a review website**: We do not publish star ratings, subjective opinions, or commentary.
- **NOT a cruise blog**: We do not write sponsored travel guides or holiday marketing articles.
- **NOT a social network**: We do not host forums, user profiles, or community feeds.
- **NOT a recommendation funnel**: We do not declare any single cabin as "objectively best."

---

## 3. Product Philosophy

Passengers care about one fundamental question:

> **"I want to understand my ship."**

Every layer of Timonelo serves this hierarchy:

```
         EXPERIENCE
             ↑
        ORIENTATION
             ↑
         KNOWLEDGE
             ↑
          ASSETS
```

- **Assets** (photos, CAD plans, specs) exist only to construct structured **Knowledge**.
- **Knowledge** exists only to create immediate spatial **Orientation**.
- **Orientation** exists only to deliver a calm, confident human **Experience**.

---

## 4. Strategic Principles

1. **We build Archetypes, not isolated ships.** Ships inherit baseline geometry from Master Archetypes, allowing spatial precision to scale systematically across sister vessels.
2. **Design for scale.** We reject bespoke one-off layouts in favor of unified geometric systems.
3. **Calm before impressive.** True clarity is the complete absence of noise, marketing hype, and cognitive clutter.
4. **Honesty over flattery.** We document obstructed balconies and structural overhangs with neutral, objective accuracy.
