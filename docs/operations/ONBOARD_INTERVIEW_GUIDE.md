# ONBOARD RESEARCH & DISCOVERY GUIDE
### Structured Qualitative Inquiry for MSC Bellissima · October 2026

---

## 1. Interview Philosophy & Ground Rules

> **Core Objective:**  
> *"We are not seeking validation or compliments. We are uncovering invisible friction, unspoken anxieties, and spatial surprises."*

* **Rule 1: Never pitch first.** Listen for 80% of the conversation before showing the app.
* **Rule 2: Capture verbatim phrases.** Exact traveler words (*"I had no idea the buffet was a 5-minute hike from my room"*) are worth 100 survey scores.
* **Rule 3: Respect crew time.** Never interrupt active duty; speak during changeovers, port breaks, or casual lounge encounters.

## 3. Inquiry Protocols

### 1. Passengers (Cruisers in Lounges, Promenade, or Solarium)
1. *"When you booked your cabin, what was the one thing you were most nervous or unsure about?"*
2. *"Did your stateroom have any unexpected surprises on Day 1 — good or bad (noise, view, walk to the lift)?"*
3. *"How do you typically figure out your daily schedule: the paper Daily Program, the onboard app, or asking staff?"*
4. *"If you could know three exact facts before stepping on board, what would they be?"*

---

### 2. Guest Services (Front Desk Staff)
1. *"On embarkation day between 14:00 and 18:00, what are the top 3 questions guests come to the desk to ask?"*
2. *"What are the most common stateroom change requests you receive during the first 24 hours?"*
3. *"Where do passengers get lost most frequently on this ship (e.g. crossing between Deck 6 and Deck 7 aft)?"*

---

### 3. Housekeeping (Stateroom Stewards)
1. *"What is the most frequent logistical problem guests in your corridor ask you to help solve?"*
2. *"Are there specific cabins in your section that experience noise from service pantries, luggage lifts, or decks above?"*
3. *"For guests with strollers or wheelchairs, what is the trickiest part of navigating these stateroom doors or hallways?"*

---

### 4. Excursion Desk & Shore Logistics Officers
1. *"In ports like Genoa, Civitavecchia, or Marseille, how many guests miss all-aboard or get panicked about return transport?"*
2. *"What is the single biggest misconception guests have about tender ports vs. docking piers?"*
3. *"What is the clearest piece of advice you give people walking off the gangway on Day 1?"*

---

### 5. Hotel Director & Guest Experience Leads
1. *"What percentage of guest friction is purely spatial (getting lost, long walks, noisy vertical neighbors) versus service-related?"*
2. *"How does the ship manage guest orientation during heavy turnaround days?"*
3. *"If you had a neutral platform that prevented guests from booking cabins mismatched to their sleep needs, how would that impact your Voyage Net Promoter Score?"*
