## 1. Design Ethos & Material Standards

> **Design Principle:**  
> *"If an item looks like promotional cruise spam, throw it away. Everything in the Founder Kit must feel like an invitation from an architectural studio or a private salon."*

* **Color Palette:** Deep Maritime Ink (`#0c1b2a`), Warm Unbleached Paper (`#f4f2ed`), Warm Nautical Gold (`#c99a5b`).
* **Typography:** `Newsreader` (Headlines & Title Serifs), `Inter` (Functional & Contact details), `SF Mono / JetBrains` (Vessel coordinates & IMO metadata).
* **Tactile Quality:** Heavyweight cotton paper, matte soft-touch coatings, letterpress debossing, zero glossy plastic sheen.
