## 1. Core Philosophy: Why This is NOT a Social Network

> **Constitutional Law:**  
> *"Timonelo does not host social feeds, public comments, or anonymous ratings. It hosts structured, peer-reviewed operational intelligence."*

Crew members live aboard passenger vessels for months at a time. They know every creaking bulkhead, every elevator choke point on turnaround morning, every tricky door threshold for wheelchair guests, and every tender boarding swell limit.

The **Crew Contribution Programme** is a professional, high-trust mechanism for maritime officers, hotel directors, guest services staff, and stateroom stewards to contribute precise operational truths that help future guests have a seamless, regret-free voyage.

## 2. Verified Crew Contributor Protocol

### 1. Verification Tiers
* **Level 1 (Candidate Contributor):** Verified active voyage assignment. Contributions enter the moderation staging queue.
* **Level 2 (Verified Specialist):** Department verified (e.g. Excursion Specialist, Hotel Officer, Accessibility Lead). Contributions receive fast-track review.
* **Level 3 (Bridge / Hotel Fellow):** Senior verified contributor with demonstrated record of accurate spatial and logistical submissions.

### 2. Privacy & Employer Protection
* Crew contributions are **strictly anonymous to the public** to protect employment contracts and corporate social media policies.
* In public knowledge manifests, contributions are attributed cryptographically as:
  `"source_id": "crew:verified-bellissima-hotel-steward-2026"` without exposing real names or employee IDs.

## 4. Why Crew Members Contribute

1. **Guest Service Relief:** 70% of front-desk and steward questions on Day 01 are repetitive navigation inquiries (*"Where is my muster station?", "Why does my keycard not open the muster door?", "Which elevator goes to the buffet without walking through the smoking zone?"*). Timonelo solves these before guests ask.
2. **Professional Pride:** Maritime professionals take immense pride in their ships. Contributing ensures their vessel is represented with architectural accuracy and respect.
3. **Accessibility Advocacy:** Stateroom stewards and accessibility coordinators help ensure wheelchair and mobility-impaired guests never book a cabin with an impossible threshold or impassable corridor.
