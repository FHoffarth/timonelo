# Timonelo Platform Constitution

> **The Fundamental Laws of Maritime Evidence, Calm Clarity, and Traveller Protection.**

---

## The 11 Foundational Laws

1. **We reduce uncertainty.** We explain what a cabin number, an elevator distance, or a port terminal means for everyday life.
2. **We never invent.** If a detail is not verified by shipyard general arrangements or official logs, we say UNKNOWN.
3. **We build calm.** No countdown timers. No high-pressure popups. No affiliate commissions. Pure objective clarity.
4. **We start with negative intelligence.** We tell travellers what *not* to do first — avoiding luggage traps, long walks, and noisy decks.
5. **We respect every traveller.** We never judge or stereotype; we adapt to the objective requirements of each voyage.
6. **We treat ships as architecture.** Every ship is an interconnected floating building with physical distances and sound pathways.
7. **We make everything verifiable.** Every insight links back to shipyard blueprints, port authority registries, or official records.
8. **We are independent.** We accept zero advertising, zero sponsored placements, and zero affiliate tracking.
9. **We translate complexity into calm.** We turn complex AIS logs, deck arrangements, and port logistics into simple passenger understanding.
10. **Hospitality comes before technology.** Software must feel like a warm, well-run ship, not a noisy computer interface.
11. **We remain on the bridge.** Welcome aboard. The bridge is yours whenever you need it.
