# 09_GLOSSARY — Canonical Maritime & Epistemic Domain Lexicon

### A
- **Adjacency (Topological)**: A direct geometric or graph relationship between two spatial entities along a corridor (*fore*, *aft*, *across*), or across deck boundaries (*overhead*, *underfoot*).
- **All-Aboard Time**: The strict operational deadline (typically 30–60 min before vessel departure) by which all passengers and crew must be physically on board.
- **Authoritative Artifact**: A primary physical or digital document (e.g. shipyard general arrangement blueprint, IMO registration, official deck plan) pinned with a cryptographic SHA-256 digest.

### B
- **BOT (Building Topology Ontology)**: W3C standard ontology modeling spatial zones, storeys, spaces, and adjacency relationships.
- **Bow Wedge**: The tapered forward structural zone of a vessel housing forward-facing suites, bridge wings, and panoramic staterooms.

### C
- **Cabin Intelligence**: The deep spatial and acoustic profiling of an individual stateroom, including square footage, balcony dimensions, connecting doors, and vertical noise buffer analysis.
- **Calculus Engine**: The mathematical inference layer that derives confidence scores, evaluates logical constraints, and calculates transitive spatial closures.
- **Canonical Data Store**: The version-controlled JSON dataset located in `data/` serving as the normalized source of truth for the platform.
- **Cruise Math**: The independent financial calculator evaluating break-even drink package economics, daily gratuity liabilities, and total voyage cost.

### D
- **Design Freeze v1**: The canonical visual language and component architecture defined in the official Figma prototype and implemented in `frontend/`.

### E
- **Epistemic State**: The epistemological classification of a statement (`KNOWN`, `DERIVED`, `VERIFIED`, `LIKELY`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICT`).
- **Evidence Record**: A verifiable provenance link establishing the exact artifact, extractor activity, and timestamp that produced a claim.

### L
- **Living Deck**: The world's first semantic, interactive spatial knowledge graph modeling cruise vessel decks based on the passenger's walking mental model.

### P
- **PROV-O (Provenance Ontology)**: W3C standard ontology modeling provenance entities, activities, and agent attributions.
- **Port Intelligence**: Comprehensive operational, logistical, and DIY guidance for cruise ports (tender vs. berth, local transport, all-aboard buffers).

### S
- **Spatial Grammar**: The topological rules governing ship layout (4 parallel longitudinal tracks, structural lift cores, atrium voids, and zone runs).
- **Stern Transom**: The structural aft boundary of a vessel housing wake view staterooms and panoramic verandas.

### T
- **Truth Engine**: The deterministic core of Timonelo that evaluates statements, resolves conflicts, and prevents ungrounded assertions from reaching the presentation layer.
