# Timonelo Product Specification

---

## 1. Product Standard: The Cabin Briefing

A Cabin Briefing is a cabin-specific record of verified spatial evidence. It explains what is physically known about a cabin, how that information was derived, and where evidence stops.

Every briefing section must answer three questions:
1. **What does the evidence support?**
2. **How was that conclusion reached?**
3. **What cannot be concluded from the available evidence?**

If a section cannot answer all three, it is not published.

## 2. The Passenger Journey

```
[ Homepage: Direct Anchor ]
         ↓ (Spatial Dive)
[ Ship Profile: Elevation & Vertical Stack ]
         ↓ (Deck Selection)
[ Deck Experience: Corridor Wayfinding & Relative Distances ]
         ↓ (Coordinate Zoom)
[ Cabin Experience: Balcony Sightline, Sockets & Overhead Sandwich ]
```

### The Seven Core Cabin Truths
1. **Bed Placement**: Where the bed sits relative to the window/balcony vs. the bathroom.
2. **Overhead Sandwich**: What physical space sits directly above the ceiling (quiet cabins, carpeted buffet, galley, etc.).
3. **Balcony Sightline**: Whether the horizon line of sight is 100% unobstructed, or if lifeboats/cantilevers are visible.
4. **Hull Side**: Port (Even) vs. Starboard (Odd), establishing sunlight orientation during sailing.
5. **Elevator Distance**: Walking distance in meters and seconds to the nearest vertical core.
6. **Electrical Matrix**: Inventory of European Type F, US Type A/B, and USB outlets.
7. **Bulkhead Privacy**: Adjoining connecting doors or shared service pantries.

## 3. The 16 Experience Dimensions

Passengers navigate through human rituals rather than database entities:

| Experience | Need & Ritual | Spatial Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. SLEEP** | Restorative darkness & silence | Quiet stateroom tiers away from galleys and lifts |

| **3. RELAX** | Decompression & ocean views | Shaded solariums, quiet aft nooks |

| **5. WATCH** | Performing arts & shows | Double-deck proscenium theatres |

| **7. WALK** | Movement & sea-breeze steps | Wrap-around promenade decks |

## 4. User Personas & Mental Models

Timonelo addresses eight primary traveler mental models:

1. **First-Time Cruiser**: Reduces scale anxiety through simple 3-node wayfinding (Cabin $\leftrightarrow$ Food $\leftrightarrow$ Entertainment).
2. **Family Organizer**: Clarifies safety, connecting doors, and walking distance to youth areas.
3. **Luxury Traveler**: Identifies privacy, acoustic buffering, and balcony overhangs.
4. **Senior Traveler**: Provides level walking distances, lack of steps, and elevator locations.
5. **Maritime Enthusiast**: Presents architectural specifications, naval blueprints, and shipyard delivery data.
6. **Travel Agent**: Facilitates rapid multi-cabin verification audits.
7. **Reduced-Mobility Guest**: Documents doorway clear widths and step-free navigation routes.
8. **Solo Traveler**: Highlights corridor lighting, safety, and direct routes to central public spaces.

## 5. Negative Boundaries (What NEVER Appears)

- NO review stars (e.g., "4.5 / 5 Stars")
- NO promotional holiday marketing or slogans
- NO urgency triggers ("Only 2 cabins left!")
- NO booking or pricing widgets
- NO retouched, oversaturated advertising photos
