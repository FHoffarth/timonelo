# 01_PRODUCT_VISION — The Timonelo Mission & Philosophy

## 1. Executive Mission

> **"Help users make better cruise decisions through verified, independent intelligence."**

Cruise travel is one of the most complex, expensive, and opaque consumer travel products in the world. Passengers face:
- **Misleading Marketing Floor Plans**: Official PDF deck plans simplify or distort corridor widths, elevator distances, obstructed views, and acoustic noise zones.
- **Hidden Cost Traps**: Drink packages, Wi-Fi tiers, daily gratuities, and port excursions are bundled with aggressive upselling and opaque break-even math.
- **Logistical Vulnerabilities**: Unclear tender boat operations, strict all-aboard countdowns, and complex Schengen consular rules create avoidable stress.

**Timonelo solves this by making knowledge the product.**

## 2. Core Value Proposition

| Dimension | Legacy Industry Standard | The Timonelo Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Deck Navigation** | Static PDF raster images with arbitrary scaling | Semantic, interactive **Living Deck** reflecting the passenger walking mental model |
| **Cabin Intelligence** | Generic marketing stock photos | Exact spatial coordinates, vertical buffer analysis (overhead/underfoot noise), and connected door audits |
| **Epistemic Trust** | Unverifiable travel agent claims | Cryptographic SHA-256 provenance pinning to builder GA blueprints with W3C PROV-O |
| **Financial Math** | Affiliate commission links and upsell prompts | Independent, zero-commission break-even calculators based on published tariffs |
| **Port Wayfinding** | Expensive cruise line bus tours | Independent DIY logistics, tender transfer timings, and all-aboard buffer warnings |

---

## 3. Guiding Scientific Principles

1. **Epistemic Honesty over Marketing Polish**: If a fact is unverified or derived by heuristic, the platform explicitly labels it `[DERIVED]` or `[UNKNOWN]`. We never pretend to have certainty where only inference exists.
2. **Topological Truth over Metric Fiction**: We do not draw fake millimeter walls. We model topological relationships: *adjacent fore*, *adjacent aft*, *adjacent across corridor*, *vertical overhead deck zone*.
3. **Zero Commercial Bias**: Timonelo operates with zero affiliate tracking, zero commissions, and zero commercial partnerships with cruise operators. Our sole loyalty is to objective maritime truth.
