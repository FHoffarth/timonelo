## 2. The Decision Pyramid

Every capability within Timonelo—past, present, and future—must strictly conform to the **Decision Pyramid**. A feature that does not move the traveler upward toward confidence is forbidden.

```
                  ▲
                 / \
                /   \
               / CONF-
              / IDENCE\
             /─────────\
            / DECISION  \
           /─────────────\
          /   CONTEXT     \
         /─────────────────\
        / VERIFIED KNOWLEDGE\
       /─────────────────────\
      /       EVIDENCE        \
     /─────────────────────────\
    /          UNKNOWN          \
   /─────────────────────────────\
```

1. **Unknown**: The natural baseline of reality. Missing facts are honestly recognized (`Unknown remains Unknown`).
2. **Evidence**: Raw cryptographic data, General Arrangement blueprints, onboard surveys, official consular rules (`EvidenceLink`).
3. **Verified Knowledge**: Epistemically sound facts satisfying the Two-Source Rule (`CANON.md`).
4. **Context**: Stateless evaluation of facts against human persona needs (Mobility, Family, Acoustics, Schedule).
5. **Decision**: The traveler's sovereign, friction-free choice.
6. **Confidence**: Complete peace of mind. The question is resolved.

## 4. The Golden Rule of Timonelo

In modern software, apps are designed as "attention casinos"—trapping users in endless scrolling and synthetic chats to maximize ad impressions.

Timonelo operates on the direct opposite principle:

> ### **The Golden Rule: Never optimize for interaction. Optimize for resolution.**  
> ### **A perfect answer ends the conversation.**

If a traveler opens Timonelo, grasps their orientation within **15 seconds**, closes their device, and steps back out to enjoy their voyage with family, **Timonelo has succeeded completely**.

## 5. The Bridge Officer as an Interface, Not the Product

It is a common misunderstanding to equate Timonelo with the **Bridge Officer**.

* **The Decision Engine** (Planes 1–4) is the product: the pure, deterministic mathematical core that stores truth and calculates certainty.
* **The Bridge Officer** is merely its human conversational voice—one of many presentation interfaces.

Future interfaces will evolve naturally:
* **Conversational Voice** (In-cabin smart speakers)
* **Augmented Reality (AR)** (Wayfinding arrows projected in ship corridors)
* **Smart Eyewear & Wearables** (Haptic turn-by-turn guidance at sea)
* **Interactive Cabin & Masthead Displays**

In every modality, **the underlying Decision Engine remains identical, immutable, and sovereign**.

## 7. Negative Intelligence: The Decisions Avoided

A fundamental tenet of Decision Intelligence is the recognition of **Negative Intelligence**:

> ### **The value of Timonelo is measured by decisions avoided as much as by decisions made.**

In traditional software, success is counted only by positive actions (items bought, buttons pressed, miles navigated). In Timonelo, true quality is measured by **the friction, mistakes, and regret that never occurred** because the traveler was given calm, verified orientation beforehand.
