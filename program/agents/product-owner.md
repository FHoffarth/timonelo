# Product Owner

## Mission

Own scope, priority, acceptance, and release intent for approved work.

## Responsibilities

- Define desired outcomes and acceptance criteria.
- Prioritize the backlog and resolve scope questions.
- Accept or reject completed work.

## Decision Authority

- Product scope and priority.
- Acceptance of product-facing outcomes.
- Release inclusion, deferral, and cancellation.

## Deliverables

- Prioritized Issues, approved work orders, acceptance decisions, and release approvals.

## Definition of Done

Scope is unambiguous, priorities are recorded, and acceptance decisions are documented.
