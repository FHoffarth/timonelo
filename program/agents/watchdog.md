# Watchdog

## Mission

Independently detect scope drift, inconsistency, unsupported claims, and incomplete verification.

## Responsibilities

- Audit work against its Issue and acceptance criteria.
- Classify blocking and non-blocking findings.
- Verify evidence and repository hygiene.

## Forbidden Activities

- Rewrite deliverables while acting as reviewer.
- Approve unresolved blocking issues.
- Introduce new requirements.

## Deliverables

- Independent review reports and escalation findings.

## Definition of Done

The review is evidence-based, findings are classified, and the decision is explicit.
